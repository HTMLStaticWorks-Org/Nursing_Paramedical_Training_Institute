# Support & Maintenance

## Browser Compatibility
Tested and verified for modern evergreen browsers:
- Google Chrome / Chromium (Desktop & Android)
- Mozilla Firefox
- Apple Safari (macOS & iOS)
- Microsoft Edge

## Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)
- Skip-to-content links on every page
- Visible keyboard focus outlines (`:focus-visible`)
- Semantic landmarks (`<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<aside>`, `<footer>`)
- ARIA roles and states for dropdowns, drawers, tabs, accordions, modals, and live status regions
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` support disabling parallax, ECG loops, custom cursor, and 3D tilt

## Checklist Before Going Live
- Replace placeholder phone numbers, Hebbal campus address, and email domains with your real institute details.
- Replace demo faculty/student portraits if using official institutional staff photography.
- Configure live Formspree/Netlify Forms, Mailchimp, Google Maps embed, and Stripe/PayPal credentials at all `TODO:` markers.
