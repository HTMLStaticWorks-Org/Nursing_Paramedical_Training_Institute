12 Hospital-Grade Training Zones

Clinical Laboratories, Simulation Bays & Residential Campus

Every laboratory mirrors active NABH hospital infrastructure so students build muscle memory in a zero-risk supervised environment.

Hands-On Clinical Infrastructure

Six Dedicated Nursing & Paramedical Skills Laboratories

Clinical Lab 01

Nursing Foundation Lab

Equipped with 18 motorized hospital beds, bedside vitals carts, suction regulators, and linen trolleys where first-year trainees master bed-making, vital assessment, catheterization, and aseptic dressing protocols.

Clinical Lab 02

Advanced High-Fidelity Simulation Lab

Wireless programmable adult, obstetric, and neonatal patient simulators with telemetry monitors, defibrillators, and debriefing control rooms for realistic Code Blue, sepsis, and airway rescue rehearsals.

Clinical Lab 03

Anatomy & Physiology Museum

An illuminated academic museum housing articulated human skeletal assemblies, cross-sectional organ models, cardiovascular perfusion rigs, and preserved wet specimens for tactile structural mastery.

Clinical Lab 04

Community Health & Midwifery Lab

Features birthing pelvis trainers, newborn resuscitation warmers, immunization cold-chain carriers, and rural community health bags replicating Primary Health Centre field workflows.

Clinical Lab 05

Pharmacology & Biochemistry Lab

Modern analytical benches with semi-auto biochemistry analyzers, spectrophotometers, hematology counters, and clinical drug formulary reference cabinets for DMLT and B.Sc. practicals.

Clinical Lab 06

Paramedic Skills Lab & Ambulance Bay

Dedicated on-campus Basic & Advanced Life Support ambulance training bay stocked with scoop stretchers, cervical collars, spinal boards, and portable suction units for golden-hour trauma drills.
