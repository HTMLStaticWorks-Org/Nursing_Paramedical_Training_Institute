1:15 Guardian Faculty System

How Our Academic & Emotional Mentorship Model Works

Pillar 01

Dedicated Cohort Guardian

Every 15 students are assigned one faculty mentor from Day 1 through graduation for bi-weekly academic check-ins.

Pillar 02

OSCE Skill Remediation

Students needing extra practice on IV access, drug math, or anatomy receive booked one-on-one simulation lab clinics.

Pillar 03

Clinical Resilience & Wellbeing

Post-rotation debrief circles help trainees process demanding ICU shifts and build healthy lifelong caregiver habits.
