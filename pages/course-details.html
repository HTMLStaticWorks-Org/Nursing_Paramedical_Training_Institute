B.Sc. Nursing (Hons. Clinical Track)

An 8-semester credit-based nursing degree combining biomedical foundations, simulation OSCE labs, and 2,100+ supervised bedside hours across critical care, maternity, pediatric, and community health settings.

Apply for B.Sc. Nursing
Program Overview

Clinical Mastery Designed for Modern Tertiary Care

Aligned with the revised Indian Nursing Council (INC) semester curriculum, the B.Sc. Nursing program transforms 10+2 science students into confident Registered Nurses (RN & RM). Trainees progress systematically from fundamental bedside care and sterile technique to advanced ICU ventilator weaning, high-risk obstetrics, and nursing research.

INC Revised Syllabus

Curriculum by Semester

Applied Anatomy & Physiology, Applied Sociology & Psychology, Nursing Foundations I & II (Vital Signs, Bedside Hygiene, Injection Safety, OSCE Lab Sign-off), Applied Biochemistry, Applied Nutrition & Dietetics, Health & Nursing Informatics.
Applied Microbiology & Infection Control, Pharmacology I & II, Pathology & Genetics, Adult Health (Medical-Surgical) Nursing I & II with supervised hospital ward rotations, Professionalism, Values & Bioethics.
Child Health & NICU Nursing, Mental Health & Psychiatric Nursing, Community Health Nursing I, Educational Technology & Nursing Education, Midwifery / Obstetrics & Gynecology (OBG) Nursing with Labor Suite practicum.
Nursing Management & Hospital Leadership, Nursing Research & Biostatistics project, Community Health II, and 22-week full-time integrated clinical internship across Medical ICU, Cardiac ICU, Emergency Trauma, OT, and Maternal Wards.
